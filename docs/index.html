What SolFit does

Counts squats with your camera

Stand side-on to the phone and SolFit tracks your hip, knee and ankle to count each full squat.

30 a day

A daily target, streaks, weekly progress and a monthly calendar of the days you hit it.

Badges

Earn badges for milestones and streaks. Claiming one sends 0.001 SOL with a memo naming the badge.

Your wallet is your account

No email or password. Your wallet signs a one-time message to sign in; it never shares your keys.

How to install

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded file solfit-android.apk from your browser's downloads or a notification.
  3. Android may say your browser isn't allowed to install apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. To sign in, install a Solana wallet app such as Phantom or Solflare first, then tap Connect Wallet in SolFit.

Privacy

  • Camera: pose detection runs on your phone. Video is not recorded, saved or uploaded; each frame is analysed and discarded.
  • What is stored: your public wallet address, workouts (reps, time, date), points, streaks and badges, in SolFit's Firebase database. Only your wallet's signed-in session can read or change them.
  • Never collected: your private key or recovery phrase.
  • Sign-in: a free signature only. SolFit never asks you to fund a wallet to sign in.

Good to know

  • Needs internet for signing in and saving workouts. It is not an offline app.
  • The first sign-in can take up to a minute. The sign-in server sleeps when idle and wakes on the first request.
  • Android only. There is no iPhone version.

Troubleshooting

  • "App not installed": if you have an older SolFit test build, uninstall it first, then install again.
  • "No Solana wallet found": install Phantom or Solflare, set up your wallet there, then try Connect Wallet again.
  • Reps aren't counting: stand side-on, step back until your whole body from shoulder to ankle is in view, and use good light.
  • "Couldn't reach the sign-in server": wait a moment and try again; the server may be waking up.
  • Check your download: the file's SHA-256 should match the one shown above.
