SolFit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Applies to the SolFit Android app (version 1.4.0 and later) and solana.digitiumlabs.com

The short version

  • Your camera is processed on your phone. Video is never recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Your account is your public Solana wallet address. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We store your workouts, points, streaks and badges so they follow your wallet. Only your own signed-in session can read them.
  • We never ask for or receive your private key or recovery phrase. Signing in is free and sends no transaction.
  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling of data.

SolFit is operated by Digitium Labs ("we", "us"), based in India. Digitium Labs is the data fiduciary (data controller) for the personal data described here. Contact: nakshatra.sharma3012@gmail.com.

What we don't collect

  • Camera images or video. Each frame is analysed on your phone to find hip, knee and ankle positions, then deleted. Only the resulting rep count is saved.
  • Your private key, recovery phrase or wallet password.
  • Your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Health records. SolFit counts squats; it doesn't collect medical information.

Stored only on your phone

Your wallet connection token and any badge claims waiting for confirmation are kept in your phone's encrypted secure storage. They are removed when you disconnect or uninstall the app.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your data tied to your wallet.
  • To show your progress, goals, streaks and badges, and to award points.
  • To record badge claims you choose to make.
  • To protect the service against fraud and abuse, and to fix problems.

We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell or rent it.

5. The Solana blockchain is public

When you claim a badge, you approve a Solana transaction: a small SOL fee with a memo naming the badge. That transaction, your wallet address and the memo become permanently public on the Solana blockchain. Neither we nor anyone else can change or delete them. SolFit also reads public on-chain data, such as whether your wallet stakes SKR, to award related badges.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your SolFit data while your account is in use.
  • You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing us from the contact above. Include your wallet address, and be ready to prove control of it, for example by signing a message. We delete your data from our database within 30 days.
  • Security records, such as used sign-in codes, are kept only as long as needed to prevent replay and abuse.
  • Blockchain records can't be deleted (see section 5).

7. Security

Sign-in uses a one-time code and a cryptographic signature from your wallet, checked on our server. Data is sent over HTTPS. Database rules let each wallet's signed-in session access only that wallet's data. Server credentials are never shipped inside the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly on any breach as the law requires.

8. Your rights

India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

  • Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed.
  • Correct, complete, update or erase your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the app and ask us to erase your data.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.
  • Grievance redressal: write to our grievance contact below. If you're not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Users elsewhere

Depending on where you live, for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local authority. We honour these requests wherever they apply. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To make any request, email us from the contact in section 1. We reply within 30 days.

9. Children

SolFit is intended for users 18 and over, because it involves self-custody crypto wallets and blockchain transactions. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used SolFit, contact us and we will delete the data.

10. International transfers

Your data is stored in India. Some providers, such as our sign-in server in Singapore, process it in other countries. We use providers with recognised security practices, and we transfer data only as permitted by applicable law.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll change the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or on our website. Continuing to use SolFit after an update means you accept the revised policy.
